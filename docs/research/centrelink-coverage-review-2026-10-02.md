## Gaps, ranked (material and flattering first)
1. Vehicles, caravans, boats and household contents are not in the assets test. Rule: contents default $10,000 (s1118(3)); vehicles at market value. Source: https://guides.dss.gov.au/social-security-guide/4/6/5/10 (via Wayback). Not modelled: AssetLine has only portfolio, super, offset, deprived, vacated home; a car ONE_OFF_EXPENSE creates no asset. Overstates pension when assets test binds: $3/fn per $1,000 = $78/yr per $1,000, so ~$40k of car+contents = up to ~$3,100/yr. Effort S (one stated "other assessable assets" figure per household, optionally declining). Needs user facts.
2. Other income streams not statable: defined benefit pension, lifetime annuity, foreign pension, trust or business income. No entry type. DB pensions assessed as full income (less deductible amount); lifetime streams bought after 1 Jul 2019: 60% of payments as income; assets test 60% of purchase price to threshold day, 30% after (https://guides.dss.gov.au/social-security-guide/4/9/3/35, via Wayback). Direction: depends; a household with a DB pension is badly mis-modelled either way (income test missing = overstates pension). Effort M-L. Needs user facts (public-sector DB is common).
3. Funeral bond / pre-paid funeral / burial plots are exempt: bond up to $15,750 each (2025-26, CPI-indexed each 1 Jul), max 2 per person, prepaid funeral unlimited. Not modelled. Direction: understates pension if the household holds one but models it as ordinary cash (assessed and deemed); size small (~$1,200/yr assets test at $15.75k, only when binding). Effort S. Needs user fact. Source: https://guides.dss.gov.au/social-security-guide/4/6/2/10
4. Younger partner's non-pension payments (JobSeeker, Carer Payment, Carer Allowance, Disability Support): not modelled. Likely small, since the older partner's pension counts against JobSeeker, but unverified here. Effort M. Needs user facts.
5. Inheritance/deceased estate interest: exempt until received or receivable, then assessed. Not modelled (no inflow entry type found). Overstates pension after a bequest; size depends. Effort S-M. Needs user fact.
6. Overseas portability: after 26 weeks abroad the rate falls pro rata if working-life residence under 35 years; Pension Supplement basic amount lost (register notes it). No overseas entry. Matters only for a move abroad or long stays. Secondary source only (National Seniors fact sheet); Services Australia URL not located. Effort M.
7. Home absence under 12 months (s11A(9)(a)): not built, VACATE_HOME parked. Do not re-offer.
8. Commonwealth Seniors Health Card: income limit $105,048 single / $168,076 couple (https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/income-test-for-commonwealth-seniors-health-card, 20 Sep 2026), no assets test, deemed account-based income counts. No cash value; benefit is cheaper health costs and state concessions. Not modelled; the model assumes spending is unchanged when the pension (and Pensioner Concession Card) stops. Direction: slightly pessimistic or neutral. Effort S for an informational flag; value is user-specific.
9. Essential Medical Equipment Payment, Remote Area Allowance, Carer Allowance, advance payment: not modelled. Small, user-specific. Source: https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/age-pension-payments-concessions-and-support
10. Asset hardship and compensation preclusion: not modelled; rare. Not relevant to a typical homeowner couple.
11. Life insurance with a surrender value or insurance bonds: assessed (surrender value); only pure-risk cover is nil. Not modelled outside super. Small; needs user fact.
12. Rent Assistance for a renter: not modelled, defensibly (Parked). Irrelevant for homeowners.
13. Granny flat interests, Special Disability Trusts, pre-2015 grandfathered account-based pensions (structurally unreachable, hard-coded false), transitional rate (no pre-2009 pensioner), Pension Loans Scheme (it is HEAS, renamed 2019; not a separate gap): not relevant to a typical homeowner couple.

## Verdict
Only items 1 to 3 plausibly move the answer for a normal homeowner couple; item 1 is the largest and the easiest. Items 4 and 5 are worth a question to the user. Everything else is rare or neutral.
