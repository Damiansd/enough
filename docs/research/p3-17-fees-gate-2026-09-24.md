# P3-17 gate — per-account super fees (read-only, @823757d)

## 1. Built vs open

**Built (slice 1 + 1b, `aaccc25`/2026-09-21):** three optional fields on `SuperInterest`
(`statedAdministrationFeePerYear`, `statedAdministrationFeeRate`, `statedInvestmentFeeRate`,
`src/core/household.ts:323-366`) parsed by `parsePlan.ts:664-734`, applied every month in
`monthlySuperFeesForOneMonth` (`src/core/projection.ts:3168-3197`), and three form boxes
(`src/app/planEdits.ts:1495-1728`). `monthlySuperFeesForOneMonth` IS called from the walk
(`projection.ts:8980`). Rules: admin `$flat/12 + balance*rate/12` (both ceiled separately), fee
deducted before growth, admin capped at balance, investment capped at balance-minus-admin, dollar
limb raised to price level via CPI, rate limbs not raised (already in nominal dollars). Absent = nil
= no line (byte-identical to pre-P3-17 fixtures, 1165/1165 proven). `core-super-fees.test.mjs`, 13
tests.

**Open:**
- **No fee cap of any kind.** `monthlySuperFeesForOneMonth` has no cap parameter — an asset-based
  rate is uncapped forever. Aware's real schedule caps at $750/yr (accum, 0.15%) and $1,300/yr
  (pension, 0.16%), both indexed. Not modelled.
- **No accumulation-vs-pension-phase fee schedule.** `SuperInterest` carries one flat
  admin-rate/admin-flat/investment-rate for the account's whole life; Aware charges different
  numbers pre- vs post-retirement ($52+0.15%/cap $750 vs $52+0.16%/cap $1,300, indexed by salary
  growth vs CPI respectively). A `RETIRE` transition does not re-point these fields.
- **Insurance limb (P3-17a) is entirely absent** — confirmed live: `grep -rn
  'statedInsurance|insurancePremium' src/` returns zero. No field, no age curve, no stop-age logic.

## 2. Measured on `fixtures/demo-couple.plan.json`

Ran `project()` twice (dist build, node scratch script) — baseline (fixture ships all three fee
fields at explicit `0`) vs an Aware-like admin-only schedule ($52/yr flat, 0.15% rate, uncapped —
investment fee left at 0, mirroring TODO.md's own Moneysmart-run convention of not double-counting
against a return assumption already net of investment fees). Alex (DOB 1971-01-01) turns 67 on
2038-01-01.

| Metric | Baseline (0 fees) | Aware-like admin fee | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Super balance at 67 (2038-01) | $776,632.89 | $755,372.83 | −$21,260.06 (−2.7%) |
| Total wealth at 67 | $909,276.66 | $888,016.60 | −$21,260.06 (−2.3%) |
| Final-month super balance (2073-01) | $359,373.75 | $323,361.68 | −$36,012.07 (−10.0%) |
| Sustainable annual spend (`sustainableSpend`) | $83,421.15 | $82,671.07 | −$750.08/yr (−0.9%) |

Caveat: since no cap is wired, this uncapped 0.15% overstates Aware's real fee once the balance
passes $500k (Alex's account starts there and grows) — so the true Aware-schedule drag is smaller
than shown, and the post-retirement 0.16%/$1,300-cap schedule was never separately applied (same
0.15% ran the whole walk). The measured numbers are therefore a directional upper bound, not a
faithful Aware replica — exactly what capless, phase-blind fields would produce.

## 3. P3-17a — age-rated insurance premiums

Source: Aware Super "Insurance Handbook – Employer Sponsored and Personal" (PDS), fetched live
2026-09-24, `https://aware.com.au/content/dam/aware/au/en/documents/member/disclosure/pds/Insurance-Handbook-Employer-sponsored-and-Personal.pdf`.

**Premiums are age-rated, confirmed, via a "Table 2: Annual death and TPD premium rates" — cost per
$1,000 of cover, before category factor and loading** (p.17-18 of the PDF):

| Age | Death $/1000 | TPD $/1000 |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | $0.26 | $0.22 |
| 40 | $0.38 | $0.87 |
| 55 | $1.63 | $4.78 |
| 60 | $3.00 | $9.73 |
| 65 | $5.42 | $17.14 |
| 69 | $7.65 | $17.14 |

TPD flattens at $17.14/1000 from age 64 (the fund's own table repeats that figure ages 64-69 —
TPD cover tapers to zero by 70 under fixed cover instead of the rate rising further). Death rate
rises every year to 69, then cover ends.

**Cover stop age:** death and TPD cover ends at age 70 ("cover ends at age 70," "the benefit ceasing
age (70 for death and TPD cover, 65 for income protection cover)," p.4 & p.10). IP cover's benefit
ceasing age is **65**, separate from death/TPD's 70.

**Inactive/low-balance switch-off, confirmed:** "we're not able to provide insurance until you're at
least age 25 and your account balance has reached $6,000. We also aren't able to provide insurance
if your account becomes inactive for a continuous period of 16 months" — unless the member
opts in (p.3-4). Also matches the fee-schedule extract already on file
(`~/.enough/verification/P13-1c-aware-soa-extract.md:75`): "insurance administration fee $22.20
p.a." = the Handbook's own "$1.85 per month" insurance admin fee, cross-confirmed live on
`aware.com.au/member/what-we-offer/insurance`.

## 4. GO / NO-GO

**🟡 CONDITIONAL GO — admin+investment fees (already-built slice) are fine as shipped; a NEW cap
slice and the phase-schedule split need explicit scoping before work starts; P3-17a insurance stays
gated separately, now with a source.**

Named conditions:
1. **Fee cap is a real, material gap**, not cosmetic — on this repo's own demo couple the uncapped
   0.15% keeps charging on an account that would have stopped accruing extra admin cost past
   $500k/$812.5k under Aware's real schedule. Any fixture with a large super balance and an
   Aware-style rate will overstate the fee drag until a cap exists.
2. **Phase-split (accum vs pension schedule) is a genuine second dimension**, not a rename: it needs
   the fields to be either re-suppliable at `RETIRE` or split into two field-sets, which is a
   `household.ts`/`parsePlan.ts` shape change, not just an engine tweak.
3. **P3-17a (insurance) is no longer blocked on "no source"** — the Aware Insurance Handbook above
   is a citable age/TPD/death table with URL and page, discharging the original 2026-09-20 gate's
   stated reason. It is still a separate, bigger build (age curve + stop-age + inactive-account
   suppression), not a fast-follow.

Smallest first slice: **add the cap alone** — one more optional field per interest
(`statedAdministrationFeeCapPerYear`, indexed like the flat fee) wired as `Math.min(...)` inside
`monthlySuperFeesForOneMonth`, absent = uncapped = today's behaviour, so it is additive and the nil
idiom holds (zero fixture churn on every existing fixture, since none states a cap today).

Fixture/golden figures that would move: **only fixtures that already state a non-nil
`statedAdministrationFeeRate` or `statedInvestmentFeeRate` on an account large enough to hit a cap**
— by the nil idiom, everything else is `+0`. Grep shows the only non-nil users today are inside
`core-super-fees.test.mjs` and the demo-couple fixture (which ships all-zero, so it wouldn't move
either) — so the blast radius of adding a cap field is that one test file, not the golden file.

Decisions the user must make before building:
- **Is a fee cap a stated-per-account input (mirrors a real statement) or a hard-coded default
  figure?** This repo's own "no default, ever" rule (household.ts:300-305) argues for stated, but a
  cap is a policy limit set by the fund/regulator, not a chosen price — closer to a `Settings`
  constant than a per-account fact. Needs a ruling before the field is named.
- **Does the phase-split need two SuperInterest field-sets, or one field-set plus a
  `RETIRE`-triggered override entry?** Changes the shape of the household schema either way.
- **P3-17a scope for a first slice:** full age-rated table + stop age, or stop age alone (cheaper,
  still closes the "held flat to 65" understatement TODO.md already names as the worst error)?
