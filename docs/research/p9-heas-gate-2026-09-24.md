# Phase 9 (HEAS) read-only gate — 2026-09-24

Scope: verify the repo's HEAS figures against sources current at/around 20 Sep 2026, list
mismatches, and give a GO/NO-GO for building P9-1/P9-2. Read-only — no `src/`, test, or
`~/.enough/plan.json` edits. Household relevance (part 3) is a separate file:
`~/.enough/analysis/2026-09-24-heas-relevance.md`.

## What's already built vs. not

**Built** (`src/data/assumptions/heas.ts`, exported via `src/data/assumptions/index.ts`, tested in
`test/assumptions-heas.test.mjs`, 20 tests):
- `HEAS_AGE_COMPONENT` — full 36-row table (`heasAgeComponent`), F2019L00420, ages 55–90 clamped.
- `heasSecurityValue`, `maximumLoanAvailable`, `personMaximumLoanAvailable` — capacity cap,
  per-Person, floor-to-$10,000, nil below $10,000 (T37).
- `heasFortnightlyCap`, `heasFortnightlyDraw` — 150% fortnightly cap, reduced by pension received.
- `heasAdvanceCap`, `heasAdvanceAvailable` — 50% **annual** cap, 2-per-26-fortnight rule (T33).
- `heasAccrueFortnight` — interest at `annual ÷ 26`, draw added before interest (T40), pinned by
  arithmetic against a published Services Australia worked example.
- NNEG existence/voiding conditions, eligibility, means-test treatment (T32/T34/T38/T39/T41) as
  documented (non-computed) `Assumption` records with sources.
- `heas.homeMarketValue` registered under `SETTINGS_NOT_ASSUMPTIONS` (no source/confidence, by
  design — ADR-0005 notes of 2026-09-03/09-04).

**Not built** (register, TODO P9-1/P9-2, ADR-0005 note of 2026-09-04):
- No `HEAS_DRAW` entry type — `parsePlan.ts` has it on the unspecified-type list (14 types
  including `HEAS_DRAW`, `DEATH`, `DOWNSIZE`), and `projection.ts` has no branch for it at all.
  `UnspecifiedEntryTypeError` fires on any `HEAS_DRAW` entry today.
- No entry-editor surface: P6-6a (Security Value prompt) and P6-6b (refinance/redraw NNEG warning)
  are both open, explicitly blocked on the missing parser.
- No wiring of HEAS capacity/draw into `core/agePension.ts` or `core/projection.ts` — the module
  is pure functions plus documentation, called by nothing under `src/` except tests.
- P9-1b (the ADR-0005 vs. HEAS market-value tension) is the one ticked item — resolved by user
  ruling 2026-09-04: Security Value is a `HEAS_DRAW` entry **param**, not a Setting, read by
  nothing but the HEAS ceiling calculator.
- The `fortnightlyRateDuringAnAdvancePeriod` ambiguity (§5.3, two inconsistent expressions in the
  register) is recorded but deliberately **not implemented** — flagged `confidence: 'unverified'`
  in `heas.ts`. Must be resolved against s 1134(1A)(b) before P9 models advances.

## 1. Sources, current at/near 20 Sep 2026

Fetched today (2026-09-24) via `curl` with a Chrome UA (servicesaustralia.gov.au blocks plain
`curl`/bot UAs with a redirect loop otherwise; a Chrome UA reaches it directly, no archive.org
needed — archive.org itself rate-limited during this session).

**Interest rate — 3.95%, compounding fortnightly.** Confirmed live today:
> "We charge an annual interest rate of 3.95% that compounds each fortnight on the Home Equity
> Access Scheme loan balance."
— servicesaustralia.gov.au/interest-rate-for-loans-under-home-equity-access-scheme (page
`dateUpdated: 20240726`, "last updated: 26 July 2024"; page itself printed/retrieved 24 Sep 2026).
Matches repo (`HEAS.interest.rate.value = 0.0395`, effective 2022-01-01, sourced to F2026L00945).

**Maximum fortnightly loan — 150% of the maximum pension rate, reduced for part-pensioners.**
Confirmed live today:
> "If you get a pension: Your combined loan and pension payment each fortnight can't be more than
> 150% (1.5 times) of your maximum pension rate. If your pension changes, we'll adjust your loan
> payments so the combined amount doesn't go over 150% of your pension rate. If you don't get a
> pension: ... You can get a fortnightly loan payment up to the full 150% of the maximum rate of
> your qualifying pension. If you are Age Pension age or older, your qualifying pension will be the
> Age Pension."
— servicesaustralia.gov.au/how-much-you-can-get-under-home-equity-access-scheme (`dateUpdated:
20230530`). Matches repo's `heasFortnightlyCap`/`heasFortnightlyDraw` (cap minus pension received).
Whether "maximum pension rate" includes supplements is **not stated on this page**; the repo's
`maximumPaymentRateDefinition` sources that to Pension Rate Calculator A (basic rate + Pension
Supplement + Energy Supplement + Rent Assistance where payable) via SS Act — UNCONFIRMED against
this specific page but consistent with the repo's existing SS Act sourcing (§5.3, primary-verified).

**Lump-sum advances — 50% of the annual maximum rate, 2-in-26-fortnights.** Confirmed live today:
> "To get an advance payment ... you must: ... have taken less than 2 loan advances in the last 26
> fortnights ... have advanced less than 50% of your maximum pension rate. You can advance up to
> 50% of the maximum pension rate of your qualifying pension in any 26 fortnight period. ... you
> can't get more than 2 advance payments in any 26 fortnight period."
— servicesaustralia.gov.au/home-equity-access-scheme-advance-payments (`dateUpdated: 20260311` —
updated this year). The page states the cap as "50% ... in any 26 fortnight period" without the
word "annual"; it does **not** contradict the repo's ANNUAL reading (T33, sourced to SS Act
s 1134A(3)(a)) — a 26-fortnight period *is* a year, so the page's phrasing is consistent with, not
independent confirmation of, the annual basis. The repo's own arithmetic check
(`heasAdvanceCap(single) = $15,611.70` at 20 Mar 2026 rates, recomputed here at 20 Sep 2026 rates
as `$16,090.10`) is internally consistent with 0.5 × 26 × fortnightly MPR. No mismatch found; the
annual-basis reading rests on the SS Act text already in the register, not independently
re-verified against the Act's current text today.

**Age-component table (F2019L00420) and the capacity cap.** Not re-fetched today (the page did not
render plain text under `curl`); relying on the repo's existing research (`docs/research/p1-heas.md`
§1, retrieved 2026-09-03, "compilation in force 11 July 2026", agreed to the cent across three
independent sources: the instrument, the Services Australia age-component page, and SS Guide
3.4.5.30) plus the register's confidence rating (`primary-verified`). This is a legislative
instrument with **no indexation mechanism** — the repo's own finding — so drift risk between
2026-09-03/2026-09-21 and today is low. UNCONFIRMED independently today; not re-verified live.

**NNEG and what voids it.** Confirmed the guarantee's existence live today:
> "A no negative equity guarantee applies to Home Equity Access Scheme loans."
— servicesaustralia.gov.au/home-equity-access-scheme (hub page). No dedicated NNEG page was found
at a guessed URL (404); the voiding conditions in the repo (s 1144AA(3): a non-HEAS charge created
or increased after participation began, with consent, affecting recovery; or fraud/misrepresentation)
are sourced directly to the Act text, which is a stronger primary source than a Services Australia
summary page and does not require re-fetching for currency (no amendment found or expected). No
mismatch found; not independently re-fetched from a dedicated NNEG page today.

**Eligibility, including self-funded retirees at nil pension.** Confirmed live today:
> "You may be able to get a loan under the Home Equity Access Scheme if you would not qualify for
> Age Pension because of your income and assets."
— servicesaustralia.gov.au/who-can-get-loan-under-home-equity-access-scheme (`dateUpdated:
20221116`, "last updated: 16 November 2022"). Matches the repo's `qualifyingPayment` row exactly
("A $0 entitlement does not disqualify — qualification survives means testing; payability does
not"). No mismatch.

**Debt assessment — assessable? deductible from the home's value?** Not independently re-fetched
today (this is SS Act / SS Guide territory, not a Services Australia consumer page); relying on the
repo's existing sourcing to SS Act s 1121(5)/s 1121(1A) and SS Guide (primary-via-archive, since
guides.dss.gov.au returns 403 to bots — noted by the repo itself). The register's stated position
(T38): where the home is the ONLY security, the debt does **not** reduce assessable assets (the
home is already exempt), so unspent drawn cash is assessable and deemed — the counter-intuitive
result. UNCONFIRMED independently today.

**Effect on the Pensioner Concession Card at nil.** Not independently re-fetched today (SS Guide
3.4.5.60, already `primary-via-archive` in the register, sourced to SS Act s 1137A). The repo's
position (T32): a full-loan (nil-entitlement) HEAS recipient does not get the PCC; a part-pensioner
using HEAS keeps it. UNCONFIRMED independently today, consistent with the primary statute already
cited.

## 2. Mismatches between the repo's HEAS figures and current sources

**None found.** Every figure independently re-checked live today (interest rate, its compounding
frequency, the 150%/50% caps and their fortnightly/annual bases, the "reduced for part-pensioners"
mechanic, the NNEG's existence, and nil-pension eligibility) matches the repo's `heas.ts` and
`docs/assumptions-register.md` exactly, including the exact dollar figures the repo derives from
the 20 Sep 2026 Age Pension rate set (`AGE_PENSION_PERIODS[2]`): recomputing
`heasAdvanceCap(single)` today gives $16,090.10 and `heasAdvanceCap(coupleEach)` gives $12,129.00 —
both derived, not hardcoded, and both consistent with the live 150%/50% rules.

Three items were **not** independently re-verified today (see UNCONFIRMED markers above): the age
component table's currency against F2019L00420's live text (page didn't render plain text; relying
on the repo's 2026-09-03 primary-verified research), the debt-assessment mechanics of s 1121(5)/
s 1121(1A), and the PCC effect of s 1137A — all three rest on primary legislation already cited in
the register rather than on a re-fetched Services Australia summary page, and none showed any sign
of contradiction in what was checked live.

One thing worth flagging, not a mismatch but a scope note already recorded in the register:
`fortnightlyRateDuringAnAdvancePeriod` is marked `confidence: 'unverified'` in `heas.ts` itself —
the register gives two inconsistent expressions for the fortnightly rate during an advance period,
and deliberately does not implement either. This must be resolved before P9 models advances
alongside a fortnightly stream in the same period.

## 4. GO / NO-GO for building P9-1 / P9-2

**Conditional GO**, for a narrow first slice — not for P9-1/P9-2 as a whole in one PR.

**Named conditions:**
1. `fortnightlyRateDuringAnAdvancePeriod`'s ambiguity (§5.3, `confidence: 'unverified'`) must be
   resolved against SS Act s 1134(1A)(b) before advances are modelled *alongside* a running
   fortnightly stream. The fortnightly-stream-only case does not depend on this and can ship first.
2. P6-6a (Security Value prompt in the entry editor) and P6-6b (refinance/redraw NNEG warning) are
   UI-layer work, not assumptions-layer — they are natural companions to the entry parser but not
   a blocker for a projection-only first slice with no editor surface yet.
3. `HomeMarketValueSetting`'s misnamed shape (noted in ADR-0005's 2026-09-04 note) should be
   corrected as part of P9-1c, not left for later — it's a rename, not new design.

**Smallest first slice:** a `HEAS_DRAW` entry type that supports the **fortnightly income-stream**
mode only (no advances), for a household using **home-only security** (the T38 case — debt
invisible to assets test, unspent cash deemed). This slice:
- adds `HeasDrawParams` (Security Value, start date, optional stop date) to `parsePlan.ts`,
  retiring `HEAS_DRAW` from the unspecified-types list;
- adds a `projection.ts` branch that calls the already-built `heasFortnightlyDraw` and
  `heasAccrueFortnight` each period, using the Age Pension entitlement `agePension.ts` already
  computes for that month (no new Age Pension logic);
- surfaces the debt as a new liability line, separate from `assessableAssets` (T38 — must not
  silently reduce it);
- explicitly refuses (not silently ignores) a Scenario combining `HEAS_DRAW` with a mortgage
  refinance/redraw, satisfying P9-1a/T35/P6-6b's substance even before the entry-editor UI exists —
  the refusal can live in `parsePlan.ts` or `projection.ts` ahead of the editor surface.
- leaves advances, the age-component annual-recalculation step function (T36), and per-Person
  halving (T37) for a second slice once the first is proven end-to-end.

**User decisions needed before/alongside this slice:**
- **The home-value input.** Per ADR-0005's 2026-09-04 note, this is a `HEAS_DRAW` entry parameter
  (Security Value), not a Setting — already decided, not open. What remains open is only the UI
  moment it's asked (P6-6a: at entry creation) and its display treatment as an unsourced estimate.
- **The security value per ADR-0005.** Confirmed: `home market value − outstanding Owner-Occupied
  Mortgage − nominated amount` (already coded in `heasSecurityValue`), entered by the user, carrying
  no source/confidence, and read by nothing but the HEAS calculator. No further decision needed to
  start the first slice; the entry editor's prompt copy (warning language) is a UI decision, not a
  blocking one for the engine slice.

**Test and golden impact:** the facility **defaults off** (register, TODO P9-1), so:
- every existing plan/scenario with no `HEAS_DRAW` entry must produce byte-identical projections
  before and after — this should be asserted directly (a test that runs the golden demo-couple
  fixture and diffs the projection with the new branch present but unused).
- `test/golden-demo-couple.test.mjs` and other golden fixtures are expected to show **zero** diff,
  since none of them use `HEAS_DRAW` today (confirmed: no golden fixture or the demo household
  references it).
- new tests belong in a new `test/core-projection-heas.test.mjs` (or similar), separate from
  `test/assumptions-heas.test.mjs` which stays scoped to the pure-function assumptions layer.
- `test/refusal-names.test.mjs` should gain the new refinance-while-HEAS-runs refusal, following the
  pattern of `UnvaluedAssessableHomeError` etc.
