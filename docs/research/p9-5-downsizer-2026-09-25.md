# P9-5 DOWNSIZER_CONTRIBUTION — TSB/TBC timing, closing the one open condition

TSB timing condition: CLOSED

- **No TSB test at contribution time.** ITAA 1997 s 292-102 (ATO Legal Database, PAC/19970038/292-102) lists the downsizer criteria — none of its paragraphs (1)–(9) test Total Superannuation Balance. TSB is not a downsizer eligibility gate, unlike NCC bring-forward/carry-forward.
- **It simply lands in the balance at the next 30 June.** ATO, *Downsizer super contributions* (dateModified 2026-04-23, retrieved 2026-09-25, HTTP 200 via curl): *"A downsizer contribution will be included in your total superannuation balance (TSB) when it is calculated at the end of the financial year"* and *"Your total super balance will reflect the amount... when your fund reports your 30 June balance."* TSB is ITAA 1997 **s 307-230(1)** — sum of superannuation interest values "at a particular time" — valued per-interest under **s 307-230A** (the provision `super.ts`'s `access.totalSuperBalanceDefinition` already cites).
- **Repo confirms no special-case needed.** `super.ts`/`p1-super-caps.md` §2–3 model every TSB read (bring-forward, carry-forward, Div 296) as the account balance at a fixed 30 June, never a per-contribution filter; `src/core/superCaps.ts`'s `TotalSuperBalance`/`totalSuperBalance()` is a bare balance-at-a-date. A downsizer contribution is ordinary money added before the next snapshot — same as any other deposit.
- **TBC:** same ATO page — counts towards the Transfer Balance Cap only once moved into a retirement-phase account, same as any other super money.
- **Age 55+** at contribution: s 292-102(1)(a). **90-day window** (or Commissioner extension): s 292-102(1)(g). **One disposal per lifetime:** s 292-102(1)(i).
- **Cap:** $300k each, couple's total capped at sale proceeds: s 292-102(3)(a)–(b) (ATO page's George/Jane, Bruce/Betty examples).
- **Non-owner spouse can contribute:** s 292-102(1)(c) ("you or your spouse held the old interest"); ATO page's John/Fatima example (title in one spouse's name only).
- **Not deductible:** ITAA 1997 **s 290-167** — *"You cannot deduct the contribution if it is a contribution that is covered under section 292-102."*
- **Excluded from both caps, not just NCC:** ATO page — *"does not count towards contributions caps (concessional or non-concessional)"*; codified at **s 292-90(2)(c)(iiia)**. **No fund contributions tax** follows from s 290-167 — never deductible, so never assessable to the fund under the low-tax-component base.
- **Age Pension once in super, over pension age: confirmed, no carve-out.** `p1-age-pension.md` §10 (servicesaustralia.gov.au/superannuation, primary-verified 2026-09-03): at Age Pension age, *"We count your superannuation both: in the assets test... in the income test under the deeming rules"* — no downsizer exception. ATO page corroborates: *"Selling your home and making a downsizer super contribution may affect income support payments such as the age pension."*
